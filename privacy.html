Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, who handles it on our behalf, how we use cookies, and how to access, correct, or delete your data.

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Nexus Global handles personal data collected through nexusconnecthk.com. That means what you send us through the enquiry form, the website statistics we collect only if you accept analytics cookies, and the basic technical data any website needs to run. It covers this website only. Nexus Connect, our separate order-management platform, is explained below.

Who we are

Nexus Global (NexusGlobal Sourcing Limited), head office Unit B, 17th Floor, United Centre, 95 Queensway, Admiralty, Hong Kong, decides how and why personal data collected through this site is used. That makes us the "data user" under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, the "responsible party" under South Africa's POPIA, and the "controller" under the UK and EU GDPR. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Nexus Global.

Nexus Global is a subsidiary of Lincor Holdings. Enquiries are read and answered by named people at Lincor Holdings on Nexus Global's behalf, which is why replies may come from a lincorholdings.com address.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry

Submitting the contact form sends us:

  • Full name, company name, and email address (required)
  • Phone number, the nature of your enquiry, and what best describes you - retailer, brand, manufacturer, or supplier (optional)
  • Your message

The site also passes along, automatically:

  • The page you submitted from
  • A country-level location and your browser's user agent string, both used only to make sense of where enquiries come from

We do not store your IP address with your enquiry. The country-level location comes from our hosting provider's network, not from logging the address itself.

Website statistics, only if you accept cookies

If you click "Accept" on our cookie banner, Google Analytics records how the site is used. For example: which pages you view and for how long, how you arrived (a search engine, another website, or a campaign link), your approximate location (city or country), your device type, browser, operating system and language, and interactions such as clicking a link or submitting the contact form. Google Analytics stores a random identifier in a cookie so that visits from the same browser can be counted together.

If you decline or ignore the banner, Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager are not loaded at all, and none of this is collected. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with what you send through the contact form.

Technical data needed to run the site

Like any website, ours cannot deliver a page without your browser's IP address and basic request details. Our hosting provider processes these to serve the site and protect it from abuse, and keeps short-lived access logs.

We also receive summary reports from search engines (Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools), our hosting provider and page-speed testing tools about how the site is found and how fast it loads. These contain totals and averages, not information about individual visitors.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry and keep a record of business communications with the retailers, brands, manufacturers and suppliers we deal with. Our basis is that you asked us to, and our legitimate interest in running our business.
  • To understand and improve the website using Google Analytics. Our basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To run and secure the site (hosting logs and the bot check on the contact form). Our basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the site available and free from spam and abuse.
  • To remember your cookie choice. Cookie law requires us to keep it.

We do not use your details for marketing, we do not sell or rent them to anyone, and we make no automated decisions about you.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. We use as few as possible, and only one category, analytics, needs your permission:

Name Set by Purpose Kept for
ng-consent Nexus Global Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies. Strictly necessary; held in your browser's storage and never sent to us. 12 months, then we ask again
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes one browser from another with a random ID. Analytics, set only if you accept. Up to 2 years
_ga_KCREDNKKMC Google Analytics Keeps track of the current visit. Analytics, set only if you accept. Up to 2 years

The contact page also runs Cloudflare Turnstile, a security check that tells real visitors from bots. It is needed for the form to work and is not used for analytics or advertising. We do not use advertising, retargeting or social-media tracking cookies.

You can change your choice at any time with the link here or at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, we delete the Google Analytics cookies from your browser and stop loading Google Analytics. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Who handles data on our behalf

We use a small number of service providers, each handling only what it needs to provide its service to us. Where a provider processes personal data for us, it does so on our instructions, as our "processor" (an "operator" under POPIA). We do not give your details to anyone else unless the law requires it.

Mezzo Collective and Mezzo OS

Mezzo Collective, a marketing and brand development studio at 1 Wootton Avenue, Botha's Hill, Durban, 3660, South Africa, built and maintains this website and provides our digital marketing and reporting. Mezzo Collective acts only on Nexus Global's instructions, as our processor. It does not decide what personal data this site collects or why, does not use that data for its own purposes, and does not sell or share it.

  • Reporting through Mezzo OS. Mezzo Collective uses Mezzo OS, its own reporting platform, to prepare website performance reports for us. Mezzo OS takes daily summary figures from Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, our hosting provider and page-speed tools. These include visit counts, traffic sources, search terms, popular pages and load times. They are totals and averages: Mezzo OS does not receive the identifier in your _ga cookie or the contents of any enquiry, and does not build a profile of you.
  • Technical administration. As the site's developer, Mezzo Collective manages its hosting, code and connected services, so it has technical access to the systems listed in this section, including where enquiries are stored. It uses that access only to build, maintain, secure and troubleshoot the site for us, never to read or use enquiries for any other purpose.

Requests about your personal data should be made to Nexus Global, as explained under Your rights. If Mezzo Collective receives such a request directly, it will pass it to us rather than act on it itself.

Nexus Connect

Nexus Connect (app.nexusconnecthk.com) is Nexus Global's separate, invitation-only order-management platform for the factories and clients we work with. It is not part of this website, and this policy does not cover it. Website enquiries are not added to Nexus Connect. They are kept in a locked table that no Nexus Connect user can see (no factory, client or staff login), which only the website's own server code can reach, and they are never used to create Nexus Connect accounts. Website analytics are not shared with Nexus Connect.

Other service providers

  • Vercel hosts the website and runs the code that receives the contact form, through a global network of servers.
  • Supabase stores enquiries in a database in Singapore, in the locked table described above.
  • Resend delivers the notification email to the people at Nexus Global / Lincor Holdings who handle enquiries, and confirms the mail arrived.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a form submission came from a real visitor rather than a bot. It sees your browser's challenge response, not your name, email or message. See Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy notice.
  • Google provides Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

International transfers

Nexus Global is based in Hong Kong, and the providers above process data in other countries, including Singapore, the United States and South Africa. Your data may therefore be processed outside the country you are in, where data protection laws can differ. We use established providers that commit to protecting personal data, including through standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards where they offer them, and we share only what each one needs.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: for as long as we might reasonably need them to manage a business relationship or answer a follow-up. There is no automatic deletion schedule, and you can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.
  • Google Analytics data: event-level data is kept for no more than 14 months, after which only aggregated reports remain.
  • Reports in Mezzo OS: these contain only totals and are kept for as long as Mezzo Collective provides reporting to us.
  • Your cookie choice: 12 months.
  • Hosting access logs: a short period, under our hosting provider's standard retention.

Security

The site is served only over encrypted connections. Enquiries are held in a database that only the site's own server code can read. Access to our systems is limited to the people who need it. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send us, and we will act promptly if something goes wrong.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, including Hong Kong's PDPO, South Africa's POPIA and the UK and EU GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your data
  • Stop or limit how we use it, or object to it
  • Withdraw your consent to analytics cookies, using

To make a request, use the contact form or the phone numbers below and tell us it is a privacy request, or write to us at our head office. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within the time the applicable law allows.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to a data protection authority, such as Hong Kong's Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, South Africa's Information Regulator, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the authority in your EU country. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Other websites

This site links to other websites, such as those of our parent company and our website developer. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it changes, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will be updated. If the change affects what you agreed to on the cookie banner, we will ask you again.
