Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers

What does Nexus Global do?

Nexus Global is a Hong Kong-based trading house and Lincor subsidiary specialising in private label development. We build retailer-owned ranges and supply product directly to existing brands, with plumbing and hardware as our core category, sourced through OEM manufacturers in China.

Is Nexus Global part of a larger company?

Yes. Nexus Global is the dedicated trading arm of Lincor, giving us the financial stability and governance of an established group while staying focused entirely on trade outcomes for our partners.

What is private label development?

Private label development is building a product range that a retailer or brand sells under its own name rather than a manufacturer's. It covers category briefing, factory selection, sampling, packaging, compliance labelling, and production oversight, start to finish.

How does a partnership with Nexus Global work?

A partnership runs in four stages: enquiry and brief, sourcing and sampling from suppliers we already know, production with pre-shipment quality checks, and delivery with ongoing support, the same team involved from first enquiry through to your next order.

What makes Nexus Global different from other trading companies?

Nexus Global's directors bring extensive first-hand experience operating within China's manufacturing base, supported by longstanding relationships rather than contacts assembled for a single deal. We work as a holistic partner, carrying sourcing, quality, logistics, and range development end to end, not handed between departments.

Where is Nexus Global based and which markets does it serve?

Nexus Global is headquartered in Hong Kong, at the point where Asian manufacturing meets global trade routes. We work with retailers and brands across the UK, Europe and Southern Africa, managing sourcing, compliance and logistics end to end so our partners deal with one accountable partner rather than a chain of intermediaries.
