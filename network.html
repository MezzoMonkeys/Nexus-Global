Network & markets

Relationships, by Design — the Nexus Global sourcing and distribution network, spanning OEM manufacturers in China, our Hong Kong head office, and retail and open-market channels in the international markets we serve.

Our base in Hong Kong puts us at the centre of global manufacturing, and our network reaches the markets our partners trade in, with an established footprint across the globe.

Footprint

Where We Operate

Hong Kong (HQ)

Our head office in Admiralty, Hong Kong is our sourcing and operations hub, positioned at the intersection of Asian manufacturing and global trade routes. From here we manage supplier relationships, quality oversight, and logistics coordination for every order we place.

South Africa (established market)

One of our longest-standing markets. We supply retail groups and brands nationwide, with deep familiarity with the country's retail landscape, import requirements, and distribution infrastructure.

Africa (proven footprint)

We support retail and distribution partners across sub-Saharan Africa and select markets in East and West Africa, each with its own regulatory environment, infrastructure, and consumer dynamics, which we plan for accordingly. It is where our model has been tested hardest.

What We Offer

Plumbing and hardware is our core category, supplied to plumbing wholesalers and hardware retailers across every market we serve.

Where We Work

  1. OEM manufacturers

    Years of orders with the same factories, not a single introduction. We know their real capability and their ceilings.

  2. Major keychain retailers

    Large-format retail groups in the markets we serve. We know their calendars, margins and range planning.

  3. Open market channels

    Wholesale and open-market routes to volume, complementing our retail work rather than competing with it.

  4. Regional retail groups

    Grocery, general merchandise and health & beauty, from our established base in Southern Africa to markets further afield.
