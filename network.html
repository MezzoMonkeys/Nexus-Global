We work with a select group of manufacturers, retailers, and market partners, relationships built over years of delivering on our commitments, not assembled for a single order. Footprint

Where We Operate

Hong Kong (HQ)

Our head office in Admiralty, Hong Kong is our sourcing and operations hub, positioned at the intersection of Asian manufacturing and global trade routes. From here we manage supplier relationships, quality oversight, and logistics coordination for every order we place.

South Africa (primary market)

South Africa is our anchor market on the continent. We supply retail groups and brands across Gauteng, the Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, and beyond, with deep familiarity with the country's retail landscape, import requirements, and distribution infrastructure.

Africa (growth markets)

Beyond South Africa, we support retail and distribution partners across sub-Saharan Africa and select markets in East and West Africa, each with its own regulatory environment, infrastructure, and consumer dynamics, which we plan for accordingly.

What We Offer

Plumbing and hardware is our core category, supplied to plumbing wholesalers and hardware retailers across every market we serve.

Our network

Where We Work

At Nexus Global, we don't spread ourselves thin. We build deep, operational partnerships across four categories of relationship.

OEM manufacturers

Standing relationships with factories across China's manufacturing corridors, the kind that come from years of orders, not a single introduction. We know their real capability, capacity, and quality ceilings.

Major keychain retailers

We work directly with large-format retail groups across South Africa and the wider continent, understanding their trading calendars, margin pressure, and range planning well enough to act as an extension of their buying team.

Open market channels

Beyond formal retail, we move product through open-market and wholesale channels across the continent, a route to volume that complements our retail relationships rather than competing with them.

African retail groups

Our retail partnerships span grocery, general merchandise, and health & beauty categories across Gauteng, the Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, and growth markets further north.
