## PAGE 3 — SERVICES & CAPABILITIES

### Page Hero

**Headline:**
End-to-End Trade Capability

**Sub-headline:**
From factory floor to retail shelf — we manage the full supply chain so you can focus on selling.

---

### Service 1 — Global Sourcing

**Heading:** Global Sourcing

**Body:**
We source across Asia's most established manufacturing corridors, with particular depth in China, and cover a wide range of categories including general merchandise, homeware, consumables, apparel basics, and seasonal ranges.

Our sourcing process begins with a thorough brief from your category team. We then canvas our supplier network, present shortlisted options with detailed cost and quality comparisons, and manage all negotiations through to confirmed order.

**What's included:**
- Supplier identification and vetting
- Factory audits and capability assessments
- Sample coordination and approval
- Price negotiation and purchase order management
- Ongoing supplier relationship management

---

### Service 2 — Quality Control

**Heading:** Quality Control & Compliance

**Body:**
Every order placed through Nexus Global is subject to structured quality oversight — from pre-production sign-off through to pre-shipment inspection. We work with accredited inspection partners in-country and apply your specified quality standards at every checkpoint.

We also manage compliance documentation for South African and African market requirements, including labelling, import certifications, and product safety standards.

**What's included:**
- Pre-production and in-line inspections
- Pre-shipment inspection (PSI) coordination
- Defect reporting and remediation management
- Market compliance documentation
- Certificate of origin and customs documentation support

---

### Service 3 — Logistics & Freight

**Heading:** Logistics & Freight Management

**Body:**
Getting product from factory to DC on time and on budget requires hands-on coordination. Our logistics team manages the full export and import chain — from container booking and documentation through to last-mile delivery coordination in South Africa.

We work with established freight forwarding partners to secure competitive rates and prioritise shipment visibility for our retail clients.

**What's included:**
- FCL and LCL sea freight coordination
- Air freight for urgent replenishment
- Export documentation and letter of credit management
- Port clearance and customs brokering (SA)
- Delivery coordination to retailer DC

---

### Service 4 — Private Label

**Heading:** Private Label Development

**Body:**
We develop retailer-owned private label ranges from end to end — working with your buying and marketing teams to translate range plans into finished product. Our private label capability spans product development, packaging design coordination, compliance labelling, and production management.

We've built private label ranges across multiple categories and understand how to deliver a product that looks great on shelf, meets your margin requirements, and holds up under customer scrutiny.

**What's included:**
- Category and product brief development
- Factory selection and development sampling
- Packaging design coordination and print management
- Compliance labelling (South African market)
- Production oversight and QC

---

### Service 5 — Brand Distribution

**Heading:** Brand Distribution & Market Entry

**Body:**
For international brands seeking a foothold in Africa, Nexus Global offers a structured route to market. We handle the operational complexity of market entry — import documentation, distributor relationships, initial ranging negotiations with retail buyers — so your brand arrives in Africa positioned correctly and supported properly.

We work selectively with brands whose offer is genuinely suited to the African retail landscape, and we act as a genuine advocate for those we represent.

**What's included:**
- Market entry assessment and retailer targeting
- Import and distribution documentation
- Retailer ranging and ranging negotiation support
- In-market sales coordination
- Ongoing stock management and reorder planning

### Geographic Footprint

**Section heading:**
Where We Operate

**Hong Kong (HQ)**
Our head office in Admiralty, Hong Kong is our sourcing and operations hub — positioned at the intersection of Asian manufacturing and global trade routes. From here we manage supplier relationships, quality oversight, and logistics coordination for every order we place.

**South Africa (Primary Market)**
South Africa is our anchor market on the continent. We supply retail groups and brands across Gauteng, the Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, and beyond — with deep familiarity with the country's retail landscape, import requirements, and distribution infrastructure.

**Africa (Growth Markets)**
Beyond South Africa, we support retail and distribution partners across sub-Saharan Africa and select markets in East and West Africa. We understand that each market has its own regulatory environment, infrastructure realities, and consumer dynamics — and we plan accordingly.
