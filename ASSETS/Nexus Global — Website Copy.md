### Our Story

Our roots go back years, hard-won experience in cross-border trade under a different name. Those relationships and that operational discipline are the backbone of Nexus Global today.

Based in Hong Kong at the hub of Asian manufacturing, and backed by the Lincor group, we pair institutional stability with the agility of a dedicated trading house.

That history is also why we don't see ourselves as a supplier. Sourcing is table stakes, what we offer is a holistic partnership: the same team carries quality, logistics, and range development end to end, not handed between departments or hired in as needed.

**Pull quote:**
Close to a century of combined experience in China: we're rarely meeting a factory, or a market, for the first time.

### Cradle to Grave

**Headline:** Cradle To Grave
**Sub-label:** A Nexus Global Added Service

**What the framework covers:**
Cradle to Grave framework manages a brand across its full lifecycle, from initial concept and positioning through launch preparation, production oversight, post-launch support and ongoing growth.

It ensures every stage stays aligned across strategy, design, quality, marketing and sales, giving clients a structured process that takes a product from idea to market and continues building the brand after launch.

**The five stages:**

1. **Ideation & proposal** — Define the product direction through positioning, competitive review, naming, messaging and initial concept development.
2. **Launch pack development** — Build the imagery, technical information, packaging, training and sales tools needed to successfully take the product to market.
3. **Production & quality oversight** — Ensure the approved design, brand identity and product specifications are carried accurately through production.
4. **Arrival & post-launch support** — Drive awareness and sell-through through campaigns, product education, retail activations and ongoing market feedback.
5. **Ongoing brand growth** — Continue building the brand through campaigns, range extensions, packaging updates, activations and digital optimisation.

### FAQ — "A few quick answers"

*Also published as FAQPage structured data; visible copy and schema must be kept in sync.*

**What does Nexus Global do?**
Nexus Global is a Hong Kong-based trading house and Lincor subsidiary specialising in private label development. We build retailer-owned ranges and supply product directly to existing brands, with plumbing and hardware as our core category, sourced through OEM manufacturers in China.

**Is Nexus Global part of a larger company?**
Yes. Nexus Global is the dedicated trading arm of Lincor, giving us the financial stability and governance of an established group while staying focused entirely on trade outcomes for our partners.

**What is private label development?**
Private label development is building a product range that a retailer or brand sells under its own name rather than a manufacturer's. It covers category briefing, factory selection, sampling, packaging, compliance labelling, and production oversight, start to finish.

**How does a partnership with Nexus Global work?**
A partnership runs in four stages: enquiry and brief, sourcing and sampling from suppliers we already know, production with pre-shipment quality checks, and delivery with ongoing support, the same team involved from first enquiry through to your next order.

**What makes Nexus Global different from other trading companies?**
Nexus Global's directors bring close to a century of combined experience operating inside China's manufacturing base, standing relationships rather than contacts assembled for a single deal. We work as a holistic partner, carrying sourcing, quality, logistics, and range development end to end, not handed between departments.

**Where is Nexus Global based and which markets does it serve?**
Nexus Global is headquartered in Hong Kong, at the intersection of Asian manufacturing and global trade routes, and works with retailers and brands internationally. Our longest-established markets are South Africa and sub-Saharan Africa, including select markets in East and West Africa.
