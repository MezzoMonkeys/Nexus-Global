Our story

A New Name, Deep Roots

Our roots go back years, hard-won experience trading into Africa under a different name. Those relationships and that operational discipline are the backbone of Nexus Global today.

Based in Hong Kong at the hub of Asian manufacturing, and backed by the Lincor group, we pair institutional stability with the agility of a dedicated trading house.

That history is also why we don't see ourselves as a supplier. Sourcing is table stakes, what we offer is a holistic partnership: the same team carries quality, logistics, and range development end to end, not handed between departments or hired in as needed.

Close to a century of combined experience in China: we're rarely meeting a factory, or a market, for the first time.

How we work

Two Commitments, Not Four Checkboxes

The same four principles that have always shaped our partnerships, paired into two things we never compromise on.

Reliability & partnership

We do what we say, from sample approval to final delivery, so our partners know what to expect at every step. That starts with genuinely understanding your business: your margins, your customers, your constraints, before we recommend a single product.

Precision & reach

Trade across continents leaves no room for error, so we apply rigorous process to every order, every shipment, every document. Our network spans China's manufacturing base and Africa's retail corridors, OEM, keychain retail, and open market alike, connected deliberately, to your advantage.

Our capabilities

A Lean, Senior Team

Everyone you deal with at Nexus Global is a decision-maker. We stay organised around three focus areas, sourcing & quality, Africa operations, and trade & logistics, not a large hierarchy.

Private label development

Our core specialisation: end-to-end range development for retailer-owned private label, from category brief through packaging, compliance labelling, and production oversight, plus supplying product directly to existing brands, not just building new ranges from scratch.

Sourcing & quality control

A supplier network built over years, not sourced for a single brief, spanning keychains and novelty items through to plumbing and hardware. Structured oversight runs alongside it, from pre-production sign-off to pre-shipment inspection, plus compliance documentation for South African and African market requirements.

OEM, keychain & open market

We work comfortably across OEM manufacturers, major keychain retail groups, and open-market channels, one network, three routes to shelf.

Logistics & freight

Full export-to-import chain management, container booking, documentation, port clearance, and delivery coordination, run with the precision cross-border trade demands and backed by established freight partners.

Africa operations

Retail relationships, range planning, and market knowledge across South Africa and the wider continent, the trading calendars, margin pressure, and shelf realities that only come from being embedded in the market, not just selling into it.

Brand distribution

A structured route into Africa for international brands, import documentation, retailer ranging support, and in-market sales coordination, backed by relationships already in place.

Part of something bigger

Backed by Lincor Holdings

Nexus Global is the dedicated trading arm of Lincor, the financial stability and governance of a group, focused entirely on trade outcomes in Africa.
