Our Story

Our roots go back years, hard-won experience in cross-border trade under a different name. Those relationships and that operational discipline are the backbone of Nexus Global today.

Based in Hong Kong at the hub of Asian manufacturing, and backed by the Lincor group, we pair institutional stability with the agility of a dedicated trading house.

That history is also why we don't see ourselves as a supplier. Sourcing is table stakes, what we offer is a holistic partnership: the same team carries quality, logistics, and range development end to end, not handed between departments or hired in as needed.

Close to a century of combined experience in China: we're rarely meeting a factory, or a market, for the first time.

Cradle To Grave

A Nexus Global Added Service

What the Cradle to Grave framework covers

Cradle to Grave framework manages a brand across its full lifecycle, from initial concept and positioning through launch preparation, production oversight, post-launch support and ongoing growth. It ensures every stage stays aligned across strategy, design, quality, marketing and sales, giving clients a structured process that takes a product from idea to market and continues building the brand after launch.

The five stages of the Cradle to Grave framework

  1. Ideation & proposal

    Define the product direction through positioning, competitive review, naming, messaging and initial concept development.

  2. Launch pack development

    Build the imagery, technical information, packaging, training and sales tools needed to successfully take the product to market.

  3. Production & quality oversight

    Ensure the approved design, brand identity and product specifications are carried accurately through production.

  4. Arrival & post-launch support

    Drive awareness and sell-through through campaigns, product education, retail activations and ongoing market feedback.

  5. Ongoing brand growth

    Continue building the brand through campaigns, range extensions, packaging updates, activations and digital optimisation.

Nexus Global capabilities: private label development, sourcing and quality control, logistics and freight, market operations and brand distribution

Private label development

Our core specialisation: end-to-end range development for retailer-owned private label, from category brief through packaging, compliance labelling, and production oversight, plus supplying product directly to existing brands, not just building new ranges from scratch.

Sourcing & quality control

A supplier network built over years, not sourced for a single brief, spanning keychains and novelty items through to plumbing and hardware. Structured oversight runs alongside it, from pre-production sign-off to pre-shipment inspection, plus compliance documentation for the requirements of each destination market.

OEM, keychain & open market

We work comfortably across OEM manufacturers, major keychain retail groups, and open-market channels, one network, three routes to shelf.

Logistics & freight

Full export-to-import chain management, container booking, documentation, port clearance, and delivery coordination, run with the precision cross-border trade demands and backed by established freight partners.

Market operations

Retail relationships, range planning and market knowledge spanning the UK, Europe and Southern Africa. Every destination brings its own trading calendars, regulatory requirements, margin structures and routes to market. Our supply model is built around those differences rather than a single template.

Brand distribution

A structured international route to market for established brands, covering import documentation, retailer ranging support and final mile delivery.

Backed by Lincor Holdings

Nexus Global is the dedicated trading arm of Lincor, the financial stability and governance of a group, focused entirely on trade outcomes for our partners.
